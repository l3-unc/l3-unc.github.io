Understanding machine intelligence through language

Language is a powerful interface to machine intelligence, but it does not always reveal how AI systems work. We develop methods to explain and predict model behavior, investigate how models learn through language and interaction, and study how language and context shape their reasoning and social behavior. Our work also examines when the explanations models produce can be trusted.
